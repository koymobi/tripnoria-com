Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Scope

This policy explains how Tripnoria handles information when you visit tripnoria.com or contact Tripnoria by email.

Information handled

Tripnoria does not currently offer accounts, comments, payments, newsletter registration or an online contact form. Email messages contain the address, name, message and attachments you choose to provide. Hosting, content-delivery and security providers may process technical data such as IP address, browser, device, requested URL, referrer, timestamp and security events.

Cookies and use

Tripnoria does not currently place advertising or analytics cookies. Information may be used to operate, secure and improve tripnoria.com, respond to messages, review corrections, prevent misuse and meet applicable obligations.

Sharing and retention

Tripnoria does not sell personal information. Information may be handled by providers supporting hosting, delivery, security and email, or disclosed when required by applicable law. Records are kept only as reasonably necessary for their purpose.

Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection and may complain to an available data-protection authority. Requests may require verification.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as tripnoria.com develops. Privacy questions may be sent to hello@tripnoria.com.
